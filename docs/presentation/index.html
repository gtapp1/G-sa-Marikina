Standout Feature #1

Barangay Polygon Map

Click a barangay. See its real boundaries light up. Only food spots inside appear.

How it works:

  • Real GeoJSON boundary polygons for all 16 Marikina barangays
  • Orange fill + outline animates on selection
  • Camera flies to fit the boundary
  • Pins outside disappear — clean, focused
  • Powered by MapLibre GL (free, no API key)

🎯 Demo moment: Click "Sta. Elena" → map zooms → Tea Kayo and Aling Beb's appear. Geographic intelligence in one click.

Standout Feature #2

AI Food Concierge

Ask in Taglish. Get real answers about real food spots.

What makes it different:

  • Speaks Tagalog, English, or Taglish
  • Only references real listings on the platform
  • Includes prices, locations, and direct links
  • Never invents or hallucinates spots
  • Powered by Gemini 1.5 Flash

"This isn't ChatGPT with a food skin. G! only knows what's real."
